Method: For a problem question, in this order: identify the source of the obligation (treaty, custom, general

prescribed textbook (Hernández) and the document reader (Elementary International Law) are both authoritative, with no

Typical errors: citing a source without explaining its content or rule; bare yes/no answers without explanation;
drawing conclusions beyond the facts given; not justifying the interpretation of a legal source; not linking the rule
to the case law as required.
