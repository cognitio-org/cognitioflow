## What the table hides

- ● *Conegate* against *Henn and Darby* is the pair to have ready: same ground, opposite outcome,
  and the difference is domestic consistency, not morality. [LECTURE]
- ○ *Schmidberger* is the one that gets over-used in answers. It is a balancing case, not a
  general licence to argue rights. [WG]
- [!] *Danish Bottles* is not authority that environmental measures always survive — the
  quantitative cap on non-approved containers failed proportionality. [WG] [READER]
