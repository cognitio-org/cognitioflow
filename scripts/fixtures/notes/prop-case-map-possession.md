# Case map — possession, prescription and good faith

*Fixture content. Not real coursework.*

| Issue | Provision | Test | Trap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Possession | Art 3:107 BW | Holding for oneself | A detentor cannot self-convert |
| Good faith | Art 3:11 BW | Neither knew nor ought to have known | Duty to investigate on a suspicious sale |
| Acquisitive prescription | Art 3:99 BW | Three years, movables, good faith | Runs only for a possessor |
| Extinctive prescription | Art 3:105 BW | Twenty years | Runs even in bad faith |
| Good-faith acquisition | Art 3:86 BW | Good faith, value, movable, delivery | Three-year stolen-goods window |

- ●● Art 3:105 is the one people forget: after twenty years the possessor becomes owner **even in
bad faith**. That is the thief's route to ownership and it is examinable. [WG]
- [!] Art 3:99's three years runs for a good-faith possessor only; Art 3:105's twenty years does
not care about good faith. The handout runs them together. [WG] [READER]
