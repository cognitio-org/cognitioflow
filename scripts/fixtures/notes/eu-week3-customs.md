# Week 3 — Customs union and internal taxation

*Fixture content. Not real coursework.*

●● Arts 28–30 TFEU abolish customs duties and charges having equivalent effect. Art 110 governs
discriminatory internal taxation. A measure is one or the other, never both. [LECTURE]

- ● A charge having equivalent effect is any pecuniary charge imposed because goods cross a
frontier, however small and whatever its label — *Commission v Italy (Statistical Levy)*.
[LECTURE]
- ● Three escapes, all narrow: genuine service rendered to the importer and proportionate to it;
inspection required by Union law; or part of a general system of internal taxation, which sends
you to Art 110. [SCHUTZE]
- ○ *Commission v Germany (Animal Inspections)* is the standard illustration of the second.
[READER]
