## Supremacy

●● Union law prevails over conflicting national law of any rank, whenever enacted. The national
rule is **set aside**, not annulled — write "disapplied" and the mark is safe. [LECTURE]

1. *Costa v ENEL* — establishes precedence over later national statute.
2. *Internationale Handelsgesellschaft* — extends it to national constitutional law.
   1. The corollary is that fundamental rights had to be found inside Union law itself.
   2. That is where the general principles line begins. [SCHUTZE]
3. *Simmenthal* — the national court disapplies on its own motion, without waiting.

○ The national constitutional courts have never fully accepted the *Internationale
Handelsgesellschaft* position. Background only. [SCHUTZE]

## Direct effect

*Van Gend en Loos*: clear, precise, unconditional. [LECTURE]

| Instrument | Vertical | Horizontal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treaty article | Yes | Yes, where it addresses private conduct |
| Regulation | Yes | Yes |
| Directive after the deadline | Yes | No — *Faccini Dori* |

> **The examinable point.** An unimplemented directive binds the state, widely read (*Foster*),
> and never another private party. When the defendant is private, go to consistent interpretation
> (*Marleasing*) and then to *Francovich*. [WG]

- [!] *Marleasing* does not require a *contra legem* reading. [WG]

## Bottom line

Supremacy is precedence, not validity. Direct effect turns on the instrument and on who the
defendant is. Both are tested as a first paragraph, not as a whole answer. [LECTURE]
