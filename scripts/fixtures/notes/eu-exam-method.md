# Exam method — the three moves

*Fixture content. Not real coursework.*

●● Applicability → restriction or scope → justification and proportionality. IRAC inside each
move. [WG]

1. **Applicability.** Which freedom, which article, is the situation within Union law at all?
2. **Restriction.** Is the measure caught on the relevant test?
   1. Goods: *Dassonville*, then *Keck*, then market access.
   2. Persons: discrimination, then obstacle.
3. **Justification.** Treaty ground or judge-made ground, then suitability, then necessity.

> **What loses marks.** Naming a case without its rule. Asserting proportionality without
> proposing the less restrictive alternative. Arguing an economic aim. [WG]
