# Week 4 — Co-ownership and apartment rights

*Fixture content. Not real coursework.*

●● Title 3.7 BW. Co-ownership is ownership of a share in the whole, not of a physical part.
Art 3:166. [LECTURE]

- ● Each co-owner may use the thing so far as compatible with the others' rights (Art 3:169), and
may dispose of **their own share** freely (Art 3:175) but of the thing itself only jointly.
- ● Partition may be demanded at any time (Art 3:178), subject to an agreement excluding it for at
most five years, renewable. [READER]
- ○ Apartment rights (Title 5.9) are the practical case: a share in the building plus an exclusive
right of use, with a mandatory owners' association. [LECTURE]

> **The trap.** "My share is the top floor" is wrong. A share is undivided. Exclusive use of a
> physical part comes from the apartment-rights regime or from an agreement, never from
> co-ownership itself. [WG]

[!] correction: the five-year exclusion is renewable, so it is not an absolute ceiling. [WG]
