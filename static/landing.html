01 — the engine

A tutor that works from your files and flags what it cannot find in them.

A general chatbot answers from anywhere. When you tick files, CognitioFlow retrieves from them, shows what it drew on, and flags some cited authorities it cannot find in them. Answers, decks and print output are AI-generated and carry the label “AI-generated. Verify every case, article and year against your own materials.”

Citation check

Flags what it can’t find in your materials.

In tutor chat with files ticked, it flags EU case numbers (C-/T-), ECLIs and article references it cannot find in those files. Article numbers are matched without the treaty or act name. Cases cited by name only, and national-law citations, are not checked. It checks that the reference appears in your files — not that the holding or the paragraph is right. That part is still yours to check.

Provenance

Answers that show their sources when you tick files.

When you tick files, answers come with the excerpts they drew on, and your files carry their type — lecture, working group, slides, reader — so you can weigh the authority yourself.

Adaptive

Revision that tracks your gaps.

Spaced-repetition scheduling brings back cards you rate Again or Hard sooner. A weak-topics list ranks syllabus weeks from your last 20 card reviews and your last two marked essay drafts.

02 — the method

Built around the structure of a legal answer.

In application mode the tutor is instructed to work through three steps and to end with a two-sentence bottom line. Print packs list the same three steps for weeks whose notes use them.

01
Applicability — does the rule reach these facts at all?
02
Restriction or scope — how far does it extend, and where does it stop?
03
Justification and proportionality — is the interference justified, and proportionate?
