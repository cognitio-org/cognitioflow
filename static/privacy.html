Transfers outside the EEA

Text sent to the tutor and to bulk tasks is processed in the USA or in places that are not region-pinned. Live dictation and read-aloud may be processed outside the EU. No zero-retention or EU-only guarantee applies today. A transfer basis under Chapter V GDPR (an adequacy decision such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for a certified recipient, or standard contractual clauses) has not yet been confirmed for each recipient. The controller has no contract of his own with Google for the router route, and no processing agreement with the router operator is on file.

How long it is kept

  • Your account, files, notes, cards and history stay in the controller’s database until you delete them or ask for them to be deleted. Database backups are held by Neon for its restore window; that window is not yet confirmed here.
  • Anthropic deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days by default. OpenAI keeps abuse-monitoring logs up to 30 days. Google may log prompts for abuse monitoring. The router operator’s own log retention is not confirmed. Retention at Google for transcription, speech and dictation is not confirmed.
  • The per-call usage record is kept while the service runs.

Anthropic, OpenAI and Google state in their published API terms that they do not train on API data by default (checked 14 November 2026). Those are the providers’ own terms; the router operator’s agreements have not been seen.

Cookies and trackers

Visiting the landing page sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser, and it loads no analytics, advertising or tracking script and no third-party font. After you sign in, one strictly necessary cookie, cf_session, keeps you signed in for up to 30 days.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to processing, and to receive your data in a portable form (Articles 15 to 21 GDPR). Where processing rests on consent you can withdraw it at any time. Write to matej@mgms.eu. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch supervisory authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl), or to the authority in your own country.

Providing the data needed to sign in is required to use the app; study material is whatever you choose to add. No decision with legal effect about you is made by automated means.

AI-generated output

AI-generated. Verify every case, article and year against your own materials. Tutor answers, flashcard decks and print packs can be wrong or incomplete.
