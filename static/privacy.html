Who is responsible

CognitioFlow is a private study app run by its owner, a law student at the University of Groningen, for himself and a small number of invited classmates. It is not a University of Groningen service. Questions or requests: contact the owner directly.

AI models

When you use the tutor, card writing or grading, the relevant course text and your message are sent to an AI model to produce the answer. Depending on configuration, this can be a provider outside the EU, and some bulk tasks (flashcards from a note) may run on a third-party model through a gateway that is asked not to keep logs. Owner to confirm before inviting students: which providers student traffic uses, and that no free model that may log prompts is used for student data.

Training data (off unless you say yes)

The app can keep a de-identified copy of generated flashcards and later edits to improve its own models. For invited users this is off unless you switch it on in your account. You can switch it off again at any time.

Your rights

On your account page you can download everything that is yours (access and portability, GDPR Art. 15 and 20) and delete your account and progress (erasure, Art. 17). The AI cost log keeps its counts with your name removed. You can also object or ask for corrections by contacting the owner, and you may complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

How long

Until you delete your account or the owner removes your invite and your data. Backups roll over within 30 days.
