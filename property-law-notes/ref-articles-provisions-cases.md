# Reference — Articles, Provisions, Cases

Headings are quoted from the project's DCFR file (`DCFR With Selected Provisions.pdf`, 89 pages: Book III Ch 5 s 1; Book VIII; Book IX Ch 1–7 to IX.-7:302). Paraphrases are compact and are not the text — quote the file itself in an answer.

## DCFR Book III, Chapter 5, Section 1 — Assignment of rights

| Article    | Heading                                                                                    | Rule in one line                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| III.-5:102 | Definitions                                                                                | assignment = transfer of a right from assignor to assignee; "act of assignment" = the juridical act intended to effect it                                                                                        |
| III.-5:103 | Priority of provisions on proprietary securities and trusts                                | security assignments → Book IX; trust assignments → Book X                                                                                                                                                       |
| III.-5:104 | Basic requirements                                                                         | right exists; assignable; assignor's right or authority; assignee entitled (contract, juridical act, court order, rule of law); valid act of assignment; no notice to or consent of the debtor required (para 4) |
| III.-5:105 | Assignability: general rule                                                                | all rights assignable unless law provides otherwise; accessory rights not separately                                                                                                                             |
| III.-5:106 | Future and unspecified rights                                                              | assignable; transfer when they come into existence and are identifiable                                                                                                                                          |
| III.-5:108 | Assignability: effect of contractual prohibition                                           | clause does not affect assignability; debtor may still pay assignor and set off, unless debtor consented / misled assignee / right is payment for goods or services                                              |
| III.-5:109 | Assignability: rights personal to the creditor                                             | not assignable where performance could not reasonably be required to another                                                                                                                                     |
| III.-5:111 | Right or authority to assign                                                               | needed when the assignment is to take place                                                                                                                                                                      |
| III.-5:112 | Undertakings by assignor                                                                   | existence, entitlement, no defences, no prior assignment; no warranty of debtor's solvency                                                                                                                       |
| III.-5:113 | New creditor                                                                               | assignor ceases, assignee becomes creditor                                                                                                                                                                       |
| III.-5:114 | When assignment takes place                                                                | when requirements met or later time in the act; future rights when all other requirements met                                                                                                                    |
| III.-5:115 | Rights transferred to assignee                                                             | primary right plus accessory rights and transferable securities                                                                                                                                                  |
| III.-5:116 | Effect on defences and rights of set-off                                                   | debtor keeps all defences and qualifying set-off against the assignee                                                                                                                                            |
| III.-5:117 | Effect on place of performance                                                             | money payable anywhere in same country/EU; assignor bears extra cost                                                                                                                                             |
| III.-5:118 | Effect of initial invalidity, subsequent avoidance, withdrawal, termination and revocation | void contract → no assignment; avoidance retroactive; withdrawal/termination/revocation not                                                                                                                      |
| III.-5:119 | Performance to person who is not the creditor                                              | debtor discharged by paying assignor until notice (or knowledge); good-faith payment to notified "assignee"                                                                                                      |
| III.-5:120 | Adequate proof of assignment                                                               | debtor may demand compliant notice or reliable evidence and withhold performance                                                                                                                                 |
| III.-5:121 | Competition between successive assignees                                                   | first to notify wins if in good faith at the time of the later assignment; debtor discharged by paying first-to-notify                                                                                           |
| III.-5:122 | Competition between assignee and assignor receiving proceeds                               | assignee has priority in identifiable proceeds                                                                                                                                                                   |

## DCFR Book VIII — Acquisition and loss of ownership of goods

| Article           | Heading                                                                                             | Rule in one line                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| VIII.-1:101       | Scope of application                                                                                | goods only; excludes universal succession, expropriation, separation from immovables, occupation, finding, abandonment, company shares, electricity                              |
| VIII.-1:102       | Registration of goods                                                                               | registrability and its effects left to national law, which prevails                                                                                                              |
| VIII.-1:103       | Priority of other provisions                                                                        | security transfers/retention → Book IX; trusts → Book X                                                                                                                          |
| VIII.-1:104       | Application of rules of Books I to III                                                              | where proprietary effects turn on an agreement                                                                                                                                   |
| VIII.-1:201       | Goods                                                                                               | corporeal movables incl. ships, aircraft, animals, liquids, gases                                                                                                                |
| VIII.-1:202       | Ownership                                                                                           | most comprehensive right; exclusive right to use, enjoy, modify, destroy, dispose of and recover, subject to law and rights granted                                              |
| VIII.-1:203       | Co-ownership                                                                                        | undivided shares in the whole; each may dispose of own share                                                                                                                     |
| VIII.-1:204       | Limited proprietary rights                                                                          | security rights; rights to use; rights to acquire (2:307); trust-related rights                                                                                                  |
| VIII.-1:205       | Possession                                                                                          | direct or indirect physical control                                                                                                                                              |
| VIII.-1:206       | Possession by owner-possessor                                                                       | control with intention as, or as if, owner                                                                                                                                       |

| VIII.-1:208       | Possession through a possession-agent                                                               | control on behalf of another under instructions                                                                                                                                  |
| VIII.-1:301       | Transferability                                                                                     | all goods transferable unless law provides otherwise; contractual prohibitions do not affect transferability                                                                     |
| VIII.-2:101       | Requirements for the transfer of ownership in general                                               | (a) exist; (b) transferable; (c) right or authority; (d) entitlement; (e) agreement on time or delivery/equivalent; generic goods on identification (3)                          |
| VIII.-2:102       | Transferor's right or authority                                                                     | transfer occurs when right obtained or ratified; ratification retroactive save intervening third-party rights                                                                    |
| VIII.-2:103       | Agreement as to the time ownership is to pass                                                       | parties may fix the moment, save where national law requires registration                                                                                                        |
| VIII.-2:104       | Delivery                                                                                            | transferor gives up and transferee obtains possession; carrier rule                                                                                                              |
| VIII.-2:105       | Equivalents to delivery                                                                             | brevi manu; notice to third-party possessor; means of control; document of title                                                                                                 |
| VIII.-2:201       | Effects of the transfer of ownership                                                                | passes within limits of transferor's right; contractual rights and third-party rights unaffected                                                                                 |
| VIII.-2:202       | Effect of initial invalidity, subsequent avoidance, withdrawal, termination and revocation          | invalid act → no transfer (causal); avoidance retroactive; withdrawal/termination/revocation not — re-transfer needed                                                            |
| VIII.-2:203       | Transfer subject to condition                                                                       | resolutive: re-transfer on fulfilment; suspensive: passes on fulfilment                                                                                                          |
| VIII.-2:301       | Multiple transfers                                                                                  | first to satisfy all requirements wins; later transferee only if unaware of earlier entitlement                                                                                  |
| VIII.-2:302       | Indirect representation                                                                             | principal acquires/alienates directly through indirect agent                                                                                                                     |
| VIII.-2:303       | Passing of ownership in case of direct delivery in a chain of transactions                          | direct delivery operates as if each link transferred to the next                                                                                                                 |
| VIII.-2:304       | Passing of ownership of unsolicited goods                                                           | consumer acquires; may reject                                                                                                                                                    |
| VIII.-2:305       | Transfer of goods forming part of a bulk                                                            | co-ownership share proportionate to entitlement; shrinkage hits transferor first                                                                                                 |
| VIII.-2:306       | Delivery out of the bulk                                                                            | each co-owner takes delivery of share; excess only if good faith for value                                                                                                       |
| VIII.-2:307       | Contingent right of transferee under retention of ownership                                         | buyer's right to pay and acquire effective against seller's creditors                                                                                                            |
| VIII.-3:101       | Good faith acquisition through a person without right or authority to transfer ownership            | other 2:101 requirements; delivery or equivalent; for value; good faith (burden on transferee); stolen goods excluded unless ordinary course of business; cultural objects never |
| VIII.-3:102       | Good faith acquisition of ownership free of limited proprietary rights                              | same conditions applied to acquiring free of a third party's limited right                                                                                                       |
| VIII.-4:101       | Basic rule                                                                                          | owner-possessor acquires after 10 years in good faith throughout (presumed) or 30 years; not the thief                                                                           |
| VIII.-4:102       | Cultural objects                                                                                    | 30 / 50 years                                                                                                                                                                    |
| VIII.-4:103       | Continuous possession                                                                               | involuntary loss does not break continuity if recovered within a year                                                                                                            |
| VIII.-4:201–4:206 | Additional provisions as to the period                                                              | incapacity; impediment; proceedings; negotiations; acknowledgement; predecessor's time                                                                                           |
| VIII.-4:301       | Acquisition of ownership                                                                            | original owner loses; limited rights known to the possessor survive                                                                                                              |
| VIII.-4:302       | Extinction of rights under rules on unjustified enrichment and non-contractual liability for damage | original owner loses recovery and value claims                                                                                                                                   |
| VIII.-5:101       | Party autonomy and relation to other provisions                                                     | parties may regulate consequences; default rules absent consent/agreement; retention-of-ownership goods → Book IX                                                                |
| VIII.-5:201       | Production                                                                                          | producer owns new goods; material owner paid, secured; not if minor labour or knowingly without consent                                                                          |
| VIII.-5:202       | Commingling                                                                                         | co-ownership by value where mass can be split proportionately                                                                                                                    |
| VIII.-5:203       | Combination                                                                                         | owner of principal part takes sole ownership and pays, secured; no principal part → co-ownership                                                                                 |
| VIII.-5:204       | Additional provisions as to proprietary security rights                                             | effective without possession or registration; priority over earlier security rights of the producer/principal owner                                                              |
| VIII.-6:101       | Protection of ownership                                                                             | rei vindicatio; declaration; protection orders                                                                                                                                   |
| VIII.-6:102       | Recovery of goods after transfer based on invalid or avoided contract or other juridical act        | transferor recovers; transferee may withhold pending restitution                                                                                                                 |
| VIII.-6:201–6:204 | Protection of mere possession                                                                       | dispossession; self-help; one-year possessory action; protection order                                                                                                           |
| VIII.-6:301–6:302 | Protection of better possession                                                                     | good faith plus right beats no right; owner-derived beats not; older beats newer                                                                                                 |
| VIII.-7:101–7:104 | Consequential questions on restitution of goods                                                     | loss/damage; fruits and use; expenditure                                                                                                                                         |
