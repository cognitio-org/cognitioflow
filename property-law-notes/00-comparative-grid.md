# Comparative Grid

The single highest-leverage revision object for a comparative course. Cells confirmed against the readings (van Vliet, Sagaert, Bartels, General Report, NL country report, Hoops); drill to cold recall.

## Land: transfer and registration

| Axis                       | France                                                 | Germany                                    | Netherlands                                                                                          | England (LRA 2002)                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What is registered         | Documents (personalfolium)                             | Rights (realfolium, Grundbuch)             | Deeds, indexed by cadastral parcel                                                                   | Rights (title register)                                                                                                    |
| Effect of registration     | Declaratory — opposability (décret 4 Jan 1955 art. 30) | Constitutive (§ 873 BGB; Auflassung § 925) | Constitutive (arts 3:84 jo 3:89 BW)                                                                  | Constitutive — "registration is the right" (ss. 7(1), 23(1))                                                               |
| Good faith in the register | Not protected — title search back ~30 years            | Protected (public faith) — positive        | Partial only (arts 3:24–3:26 BW); art. 3:88 does not cure a predecessor's lack of power — "negative" | Registered proprietor has power to dispose regardless (s. 23); possession protects against rectification (s. 65, Sched. 4) |
| Title theory for land      | Causal                                                 | Abstract                                   | Causal (valid title + real agreement in the deed + registration)                                     | Abstract in effect (only registration matters)                                                                             |
| Form                       | Authentic act for registration                         | Notarial contract (§ 311b) + Auflassung    | Notarial deed of conveyance (art. 3:31 BW)                                                           | Signed, witnessed deed                                                                                                     |
| Priority notice            | Provisional registration (3 yrs)                       | Vormerkung § 883 BGB                       | Art. 7:3 BW (6 months)                                                                               | Official search priority period                                                                                            |
| Rank of limited rights     | Time of registration                                   | Time of registration                       | Time of filing (art. 3:19 BW); rangregeling art. 3:262                                               | Time of registration                                                                                                       |

> Trap: abstract ⇒ constitutive and declaratory ⇒ causal both hold, but causal + constitutive exists (NL, Austria). Constitutive does not imply positive (NL, Greece).

## The two gatekeepers (Salomons)

|                       | Good-faith acquisition — VIII.-3:101                                                        | Acquisitive prescription — VIII.-4:101                                                |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Policy goal           | Protect commerce (reduce investigation costs)                                               | Legal certainty (broader — even absent commerce)                                      |
| Good faith about      | Transferor's lack of title                                                                  | One's own lack of title                                                               |
| When                  | At the moment ownership would pass — mala fides superveniens non nocet                      | Throughout the whole period                                                           |
| Burden of proof       | On the acquirer (unusual)                                                                   | Good faith presumed                                                                   |
| Value                 | Required                                                                                    | Not required (donee protected)                                                        |
| Delivery / possession | Delivery or equivalent — no constructive delivery                                           | Continuous possession as owner-possessor                                              |
| Stolen goods          | Excluded unless transferor acted in the ordinary course of business; cultural objects never | Thief himself excluded; others 10 / 30 years (cultural objects 30 / 50 — VIII.-4:102) |
| Period                | Instant                                                                                     | 10 years (good faith) / 30 years                                                      |
