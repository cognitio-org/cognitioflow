# Disambiguation Pairs

One line each side. Confusing a pair is a **WRONG_TOOL** error.

| Pair                                                          | Left                                                                                                                   | Right                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Causal vs abstract                                            | valid legal ground required — a void causa kills the transfer (FR, NL, DCFR)                                           | real agreement independent — a void contract need not defeat the transfer; transferor left with an unjustified-enrichment claim (DE)  |
| Consensual vs tradition                                       | ownership passes at contract, solo consensu (FR; SGA default)                                                          | delivery (or an equivalent) required (DE, NL, DCFR default)                                                                           |
| Property vs personal right                                    | absolute, erga omnes, follows the thing, priority in insolvency                                                        | relative, inter partes, binds only the counterparty                                                                                   |
| Ownership vs possession                                       | the most comprehensive right (VIII.-1:202)                                                                             | factual control with intention (VIII.-1:205–1:206)                                                                                    |
| Possession vs limited right                                   | a fact — control as, or as if, owner                                                                                   | a right to use/enjoy carved out of ownership (usufruct, lease as a real right)                                                        |
| Original vs derivative acquisition                            | fresh title, no predecessor — nemo dat irrelevant (production, prescription, good-faith acquisition per DCFR Comments) | from a predecessor — nemo dat and the power to dispose apply (transfer)                                                               |
| Good-faith acquisition vs acquisitive prescription            | good faith at the moment of acquisition; instant; for value; burden on acquirer                                        | time + possession; good faith throughout (presumed) or 30 years; no value needed                                                      |
| Numerus clausus vs specificity                                | closed list of rights (Typenzwang / Typenfixierung)                                                                    | rights attach only to specific, identified objects (Goldcorp; VIII.-2:101(3))                                                         |
| Typenzwang vs Typenfixierung                                  | which property rights may exist                                                                                        | what content they may have                                                                                                            |
| Negative/deeds vs positive/title register                     | records deeds, does not cure defects, limited or no protection of good faith (NL, FR)                                  | register determines title; public faith / state guarantee (DE Grundbuch, England LRA 2002)                                            |
| Constitutive vs declaratory registration                      | no registration, no transfer (DE, NL, England)                                                                         | transfer between the parties without it; registration for opposability to third parties (FR)                                          |
| Nemo dat vs nemo plus                                         | can't transfer what you don't own (transfer / good-faith-acquisition axis)                                             | can't grant more than you hold (limited-rights axis; encumbered ownership passes encumbered)                                          |
| Priority principle vs nemo plus                               | two limited rights of different persons on one object — rank by date                                                   | transferor had nothing left to give (double sale) — not a priority case                                                               |
| Acquisitive vs extinctive prescription                        | possessor gains ownership (VIII.-4:101; art. 3:99 BW)                                                                  | claim is time-barred (art. 3:306 BW) — NL links the two via art. 3:105                                                                |
| Droit de suite vs prior tempore                               | the right survives later acquirers of the thing (Q10)                                                                  | the right yields to rights created earlier (Q11)                                                                                      |
| Deprivation vs control of use (A1P1)                          | taking ownership — public interest, lawful, compensation case-law applies                                              | regulating how property is used — wide margin; Pye v UK classified adverse possession here                                            |
| The debt vs the security                                      | personal, stays with the debtor                                                                                        | proprietary, follows the thing                                                                                                        |
| Avoidance vs termination (DCFR)                               | retroactive — ownership/claim treated as never passed (VIII.-2:202; III.-5:118(3))                                     | no retroactive proprietary effect — must be re-transferred (VIII.-2:202(3); III.-5:118(4))                                            |
| Creation vs effectiveness (Book IX)                           | security right created by granting — IX.-2:101, 2:105 (no delivery)                                                    | effective against third parties only via registration/possession/control — IX.-3:101–3:102; ranking follows effectiveness (IX.-4:101) |
| Proprietary vs obligatory effect of an anti-assignment clause | none — the claim still passes (III.-5:108(1))                                                                          | debtor may still pay the assignor and set off (III.-5:108(2)), save trade receivables (5:108(3))                                      |
| Causal/abstract vs civil law/common law                       | both are civilian positions (FR/NL causal; DE abstract)                                                                | the common law sits on neither axis (relative title; SGA intention)                                                                   |
